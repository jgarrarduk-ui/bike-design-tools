Design

How it works

Lay out a pump track or a dirt jump line, see its height profile, and take the dimensions, GPS line and a build sheet out to the site. Everything is in metres. Your work saves itself in this browser as you go.

Start

  • Templates loads a starting track: ovals, a figure eight, a flow loop, jump lines. It replaces what is on the plan; Undo brings it back.
  • Loop or Line on the right sets whether the track joins up. Set the track width there too.

Lines

  • A design can hold several lines: a pump track and a jump line on the same hill, say. They are listed at the top right.
  • New track adds a loop where you are looking; Loop / Line below it switches it to a line. Tap a line on the plan, or its row, to edit it; the others fade back.
  • The swatch sets a line's colour; tap the name to rename it. Templates, the profile, the panel and the CSV and build sheet exports work on the line you are editing. Save and GPX carry every line.

Shape the track

  • The track is straights joined by corners. Each numbered handle is the centre of a corner's circle; the track wraps round it. Drag a handle and only that corner moves.
  • Tap a corner to change its radius, berm height or type: Berm (banked), Flat, Auto (banked once it turns more than 35°) or Hip.
  • + on a straight adds a corner. Drag it out and it bends the track, growing to a 4 m radius. Double-clicking a straight adds one too.
  • Hold a corner or a feature still for half a second and a red × appears. Tap it to delete.
  • Two corners turning opposite ways pushed so close their circles overlap are shrunk to fit and outlined red.

Add features

  • Drag a tile from the left onto a straight, or tap a tile and then tap the track. Rollers, table tops, doubles, step ups and downs, drops, a wooden drop and a start hill go on straights.
  • Berm and Berm + roller go on a corner. Hip on a corner turns it into a hip jump; on a straight it adds a 45° hip there.
  • Tap a feature to set its size (S, M, L, XL) or each dimension. The diagram shows its side view. Drag it along the track to move it.
  • Features keep their place on their straight as you reshape the track.

Ground and height

  • The strip under the plan is the side view along the track. The shaded area is the ground; features sit on top of it.
  • Set the ground height at the start and end on the right (a loop ends where it started). In between it runs in a straight line.
  • In the strip, tap the dashed ground line to add a point, drag a point up, down or along, and hold one to remove it. Squares are the start and end.
  • With a GPX trace loaded, its heights show as blue dots in the strip wherever the walk passed within 5 m of the line. Drag your ground points to match them, ignoring obvious GPS jumps. The trace layer's eye shows and hides them too.
  • Heights are metres above the site ground level set under Export (the first trace with heights sets it, if it was 0). Lip and landing angles are measured from the slope a feature is built on.

Read the plan

  • Colours mean the same everywhere: amber goes up, green comes down, pale is a flat deck, near black is a gap you fly over, brown is timber.
  • The profile strip marks the corners. Heights are exaggerated to fit; the factor is in its header.
  • Dims shows the length of every straight and the radius of every corner.
  • A ! badge top right means something doesn't fit or overlaps. Tap it for the list; tap an entry to go to it.
  • On the right: length, footprint, a rough dirt volume and the feature count.

Layers

  • The stacked squares above the zoom buttons open Layers. The eye shows or hides each one.
  • Photo: load an aerial photo or site plan, set its scale from a distance you know, and draw over it.
  • GPX trace: load a ride or a walk round the site as a reference line, at true scale.
  • Design is the track itself. Hidden, it can't be edited. Labels are the corner numbers and + handles.

Files

  • Save downloads one zip: the design, the site photo and the GPX trace exactly as you loaded them. Keep it anywhere (Dropbox, Drive, iCloud, email) and Open it to carry on. Open also reads a design .json, a GPX and a dimensions CSV.
  • Export: a build sheet (A4 PNG with plan, profile and dimensions), a dimensions CSV for a spreadsheet, a profile CSV (height every 25 cm), and GPX for a phone or GPS on site once you set the site location.

Keys

  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Ctrl+S save.
  • Arrow keys nudge the selection 10 cm, with Shift 1 m. Delete removes it, Esc lets go.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag empty ground to pan.

Sizes

Default sizes follow common build guidance, not a standard: rollers about 0.3–0.8 m high with crests roughly ten times their height apart, berm radius around 3 m for a pumpable 180°, lips 25–45°, landings 25–30°. The dirt figure is a rough builder's quantity. Check the design with whoever will build and ride it.
