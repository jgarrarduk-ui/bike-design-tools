Frame tubes

Gap from each pivot body to the nearest tube wall, so a boss does not end up inside a tube. The main pivot's two offsets and the down tube to seat tube angle are what its printed housing is made to, and change with every frame size.

Pivot loads at sag

Static, in plane, from the rear wheel load alone. No braking or cornering case yet.

Seat stay stress

Steel tubing, constant section, load shared by two stays. Out of plane bending from the lean does not cancel against the flex, so the two are combined as a resultant moment.
